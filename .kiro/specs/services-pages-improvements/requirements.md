# Services Pages — Requirements

## Overview
Fix and polish the Services page (/services) and Service Detail pages (/services/:slug) across three dimensions: layout, functionality, and visual design.

## Requirements

1. Service detail pages must use a full-width single-column layout — no sidebar
2. The breadcrumb trail (Home > Services > [Service Name]) must appear above the h1 on every service detail page
3. The service accordion on /services must stay open once expanded — it should not collapse when clicking the same item again
4. Stat counters must animate correctly (counterUp library)
5. Both /services and /services/:slug must have the dark blue radial gradient background on the hero/breadcrumb section
6. The h1 on /services must have the rr_title_anim split-text entrance animation
7. The /services page tagline must read: "Brand, Marketing, and Creative Services That Drive Business Growth."
8. Service numbers (001. 002. etc) must be visually prominent in the accordion rows on /services, matching the Home page pattern
